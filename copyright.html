YouTube Studio "Checks" Best Practice

Hamesha Video Unlisted Upload Karein: Jab aap YouTube par video upload karte hain, toh publish karne se pehle **"Checks"** ka step aata hai. Is step mein Content ID aur Ad Suitability automated scan hoti hai.

Rule: Hamesha video ko 15-20 minutes ke liye **Unlisted** ya **Private** rakhein. Agar Content ID claim ya copyright flag aa jaye, toh video ko delete kiye bina audio replace ya trim kar sakte hain. Public karne ki jaldi kabhi mat karein!

Facebook Meta Rights Manager & Originality

Facebook par **Unoriginal Content Policy** sab se bara khatra hai. Agar video mein 60% se ziada footage kisi aur ki hai ya aapka face/voice nahi hai, toh Creator Studio mein monetization red ho jati hai.

Rule: Apni original storytelling, picture-in-picture facecam reaction, aur custom subtitles zaroor add karein.

TikTok Commercial Music & Audio Inspector

TikTok par agar aap Business Account use kar rahe hain, toh trending personal songs use karne par audio mute ho jata hai.

Rule: TikTok Commercial Music Library (CML) se hi tracks select karein taake copyright strike ya audio mute hone se bach sakein.
